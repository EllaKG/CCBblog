We are a professional women's cycling team dedicated to excellence and teamwork while racing internationally and committing to a full academic schedule.

This website will showcase our everyday lives as professional athletes and blogs about our pursuit of the World Tour cycling dream.

CCB Women’s Cycling is an American professional road racing team based out of Boston Massachusetts. The women’s version of this team is relatively new within the last 4 years, however the CCB title team has been around for 50 years as of 2026, making it one of the longest running elite teams not only in the nation, but the world. This team is known for its bright pink and blue colors are streak across professional road pelotons all over the globe. However, the goal of this team is not just to win, but to develop young riders for a successful career in both the World Tour field, as well as academic classroom. Every team member is required to be at least a full-time secondary education student or have already acquired an undergraduate degree. The goal is to prioritize not just excellence in sport within the Women’s field but also set up these young women to be successful in any pursued avenue of life. To learn more about the current team members or of the mission this team aims to achieve, go to https://www.pinkwatts.com/.
